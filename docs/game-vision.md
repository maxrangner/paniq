# Game vision

## Premise

Paniq is a darkly comedic crowd-simulation puzzle game. Players manipulate a
population indirectly, spend limited influence, and watch small interventions
become surprising chains of social and environmental reactions.

## Player fantasy

The player is not a person in the crowd. They are a mischievous, limited
influence over a living situation: provoke, amplify, suppress, or redirect a
response, then adapt to the consequences.

## Design pillars

1. **Indirect control:** powers affect situations and groups, not direct unit
   orders.
2. **Readable emergence:** chains should surprise the player while retaining a
   clear explanation of what caused them.
3. **Personality matters:** the same event can create different reactions based
   on temperament, context, and relationships.
4. **Short replayable scenarios:** authored puzzle levels reward observation,
   experimentation, and alternate solutions.
5. **Dark comedy without cruelty:** escalating everyday chaos is the tone; the
   game avoids celebrating realistic harm.

## Product shape

The long-term game combines a campaign of small authored scenarios with a
free-play sandbox. The first playable work is a vertical slice; level design
and sandbox breadth follow after that slice is proven and refined.

## Not in the foundation

Networking, open-world simulation, procedural cities, monetization, purchased
assets, and mobile deployment are not foundation requirements.
